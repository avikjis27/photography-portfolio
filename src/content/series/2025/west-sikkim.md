High in the western district of Sikkim, where the formidable Kanchenjunga massif commands the horizon, winter brings a sharp, quiet stillness to the ancient mountain passes.

Walking along high monastery trails draped in fluttering prayer flags, the air is thin, cold, and immensely clear. Frozen streams trickle gently beneath thin sheets of ice, while snow-laden pine trees frame distant snow peaks bathed in golden morning alpenglow.
